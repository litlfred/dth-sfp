step lane skill / sub-process what it does
Open reporting app & check registration (DE1, DE3)
Task_1_OpenAppAndCheckReg		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 1-2 (Table 10, Figure 4): Open the SF reporting system (web/mobile/USSD) and query the National Product Catalogue (dth-pc) by product name, brand, or GTIN (SFR.A1.DE3, SFR.FXNREQ.006).
Select product & auto-populate type (SFR.DT.02)
Task_3_EnterProductDetails		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 3 (Table 10, Figure 4): Select the registered product record and automatically populate product type (SFR.A1.DE5) and manufacturer (SFR.A1.DE4) via decision table SFR.DT.02 (SFR.FXNREQ.006).
Record unregistered product details (F2)
Task_FlagUnregistered		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 2a (Figure 4 bypass branch, Finding F2/F5): Record free-text product name, manufacturer claim, and unregistered/unlicensed status when the product is absent from the National Product Catalogue.
Enter or scan batch (DE6) & expiry (DE7)
Task_4_EnterBatchExpiry		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 4 (Table 10, Figure 4): Enter or scan the product batch/lot number (SFR.A1.DE6) and expiration date (SFR.A1.DE7) via manual entry or barcode scanner (SFR.FXNREQ.007).
Select channel & SF category (DE2, DE8)
Task_5_SelectReportType		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 5 (Table 10, Figure 4): Record report communication mode (SFR.A1.DE2) and select suspected SF report type (SFR.A1.DE8, decision table SFR.DT.01).
Describe quality defect or ADR (DE9)
Task_6a_SubstandardDetails		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 6a (Table 10, Figure 4 substandard branch): Describe suspected quality defects, packaging anomalies, lack of therapeutic effect, or adverse reactions (SFR.A1.DE9).
Describe suspected falsification (DE9)
Task_6b_FalsifiedDetails		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 6b (Table 10, Figure 4 falsified branch): Describe suspected counterfeit features, tampered seals, altered labels, or illicit supply source (SFR.A1.DE9).
Attach product & packaging photos
Task_7_AttachPhotos		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 7a/7b (Table 10, Figure 4): Attach photographs of the suspected product, label, batch mark, and packaging in supported image formats (SFR.FXNREQ.002).
Confirm profile, region & contact (DE10)
Task_8a_EnterContact		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 8a (Table 10, Figure 4): Confirm reporter profile, auto-assign geographic region via SFR.DT.03 (SFR.FXNREQ.001), and record reporter contact information (SFR.A1.DE10).
Omit personal contact details
Task_8b_RemainAnonymous		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 8b (Table 10, Figure 4): Submit report without personal identifier or contact details while preserving regional origin metadata.
Submit SF report to NMRA
Task_9_SubmitReport		 Reporter (SFP-R-01..12, R-14) — Step 9 (Table 10, Figure 4): Transmit the completed SF report online or queue via the encrypted offline outbox workflow (SF-REP-006).
Receive report, assign tracking ID & log
Task_10_ReceiveAndValidate		 NMRA & Reporting System (SFP-R-13) — Step 10 (Table 10, SFR.FXNREQ.003-004): Validate mandatory data elements (DE1-DE10), assign a unique report ID (DE1) and tracking number, and store in the NMRA repository.
Return confirmation & tracking ID
Task_11_SendAcknowledgement		 NMRA & Reporting System (SFP-R-13) — Step 11 (Table 10, SFR.FXNREQ.003-004): Send immediate submission confirmation and tracking number to the reporter.
Screen report & trigger triage (SF-REP-002)
Task_12_InitialScreening		 NMRA & Reporting System (SFP-R-13) — Step 12 (Table 10, SFR.FXNREQ.005): Notify the NMRA focal point in real time and initiate regulatory screening and triage (SF-REP-002 / SF-REP-003).
