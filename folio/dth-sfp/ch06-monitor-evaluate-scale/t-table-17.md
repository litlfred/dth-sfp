### Table 17. Key steps for developing an M&E plan (p. 43 / PDF p. 55)

| Evaluation domain | Sample questions |
| --- | --- |
| Usability | • Is the substandard and falsified reporting application usable by the targeted end user(s), and does it fit within their workflow?<br>• How steep is the learning curve before a user can demonstrate proficient use of the reporting application?<br>• What are the rates of error – in using the system or in workflows – as a result of system use/misuse? |
| Efficacy | • Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed processes (e.g. time between event X and response Y) in a research or controlled setting such as a pilot?<br>• Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed outcomes (e.g. increased reporting by health care professionals) in a research or controlled setting such as a pilot? |
| Effectiveness | • Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed processes (e.g. time between event X and response Y) in a non-research setting?<br>• Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed outcomes (e.g. increased reporting by health care professionals) in a non-research setting? |
| Cost | • Has the substandard and falsified reporting application reduced costs associated with substandard and falsified reporting or substandard and falsified medical product incidences in general?<br>• Has the substandard and falsified reporting application introduced costs that are commensurate with benefits provided? |
