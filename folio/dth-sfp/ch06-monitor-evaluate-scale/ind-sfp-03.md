### SFP.IND.03 — Stability measures (system uptime, crash rate and offline sync reliability) (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56)

- **Indicator ID:** `SFP.IND.03`
- **Description:** Technical stability of the SF reporting platform, measured by system availability/uptime rate, error/crash frequency, and successful synchronisation rate of offline outbox reports (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56). No empirical target values are asserted.
- **Numerator:** System operational uptime hours and successfully synchronised reports
- **Denominator:** Total scheduled operational hours and total queued reports
- **Empirical Targets:** None (per `litlfred/dth-sfp#1` §2.3 & §5 rule: indicator definitions carry no empirical targets or numbers).
