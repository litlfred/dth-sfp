### SFP.IND.01 — Quality measures (completeness, accuracy and timeliness of SF reports) (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56)

- **Indicator ID:** `SFP.IND.01`
- **Description:** Proportion of submitted SF reports with all mandatory core data elements (SFR.A1.DE1–DE10) completed accurately, and user satisfaction with reporting ease and clarity (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56). No empirical target values are asserted.
- **Numerator:** Number of submitted SF reports with complete and validated core data elements (DE1–DE10)
- **Denominator:** Total number of SF reports submitted in the reporting period
- **Empirical Targets:** None (per `litlfred/dth-sfp#1` §2.3 & §5 rule: indicator definitions carry no empirical targets or numbers).
