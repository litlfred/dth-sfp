### SFP.IND.04 — Functionality measures (feature utilization and interoperability performance) (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56)

- **Description:** Extent to which core functional features (barcode/product lookup SFR.DT.02, auto-region SFR.DT.03, photo upload, tracking number generation, registry/GSMS exchange) perform as specified (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56). No empirical target values are asserted.

- **Denominator:** Total deployed functional and interoperability capabilities
- **Empirical Targets:** None (per `litlfred/dth-sfp#1` §2.3 & §5 rule: indicator definitions carry no empirical targets or numbers).
