### SFP.IND.02 — Fidelity measures (adherence to reporting workflows and detection-to-notification time) (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56)

- **Indicator ID:** `SFP.IND.02`
- **Description:** Adherence of system implementation to defined workflows (SF-REP-001–006), including elapsed detection-to-notification time and proportion of reports receiving acknowledgement and follow-up feedback (Table 18, p. 44 / PDF p. 56). No empirical target values are asserted.
- **Numerator:** Elapsed time from detection/submission to NMRA notification and count of reports completing the feedback loop
- **Denominator:** Total submitted SF reports requiring regulatory screening and feedback
- **Empirical Targets:** None (per `litlfred/dth-sfp#1` §2.3 & §5 rule: indicator definitions carry no empirical targets or numbers).
