### Table 3. Assessing digital readiness and enabling environment (p. 11 / PDF p. 23)

| Area | Actions and guiding questions |
| --- | --- |
| Assessing network coverage, connectivity and electricity | • Obtain or calculate the World Economic Forum Network Readiness Score.<br>• Obtain the availability of electricity at the facility level, including the availability for 8 hours a day.<br>• Obtain the percentage of health care facilities with internet access.<br>• Obtain the number of mobile phone users per health care facility.<br><br>**Guiding questions**<br>• What is the affordability and quality of internet across the country, especially in urban versus rural areas? Do they vary across provinces or regions?<br>• Do the existing 3G and 4G stations sufficiently cover the population?<br>• What is the number of mobile broadband users and internet subscriptions?<br>• Does the prevalence of smartphones exceed access to computers with wired internet connections? |
| Hardware and software availability | • Obtain the proportion of health care facilities with functioning and accessible mobile devices.<br>• Assess in-country data storage centres, servers or cloud-based storage availability.<br>• Explore the national hardware inventory.<br>• Explore the inventory of systems and platforms.<br>• Explore penetration of computers and high-speed internet, mobile phones and other smart devices in health care facilities and the general public. |
| Infrastructure | • What national, state and regional computing infrastructure exists across the country, including components such as server farms, data centres, support systems and personnel, etc.?<br>• Can existing infrastructure components scale up to support broader national use?<br>• Have there been large-scale e-health or other information and communication technology (ICT) infrastructure projects that have failed or stalled? If so, what were the primary reasons? |
| Security | • Are there robust cybersecurity measures in place to protect digital infrastructure, systems and data?<br>• Is there a comprehensive incident response plan in case of security breaches?<br>• Are regular security audits conducted to identify vulnerabilities and mitigate risks?<br>• Are there laws and regulations in place to address cybercrime and protect against unauthorized access? |
| Privacy | • Are there legal frameworks and regulations in place to safeguard individuals' privacy rights?<br>• Is there awareness among citizens about their privacy rights and how their data are being used?<br>• Are organizations required to obtain consent before collecting and processing personal data?<br>• Is there a designated authority responsible for overseeing privacy-related issues? |
| Data ownership | • Are there clear laws and regulations defining data ownership rights?<br>• Do individuals have control over their personal data and the ability to access, modify or delete it?<br>• Are there mechanisms in place to ensure organizations do not unlawfully claim ownership of user-generated data?<br>• Are there guidelines for data sharing and collaboration between different entities while respecting data ownership? |
