### Table 4. Questions for assessing current SF and PV reporting practices (p. 12 / PDF p. 24)

| Area | Actions and guiding questions |
| --- | --- |
| Assessing data collection for pharmacovigilance | • Confirm whether there is a standard adverse drug reaction reporting form in your country.<br>• Confirm whether there is a standard adverse drug reaction reporting form available to the general public.<br>• If there is a form, identify whether it contains relevant fields to report suspected medication errors.<br>• Identify whether there are relevant fields to report suspected substandard and falsified medical products.<br>• Identify whether there are relevant fields to report therapeutic failure or inefficacy. |
| Assessing data collection for substandard and falsified medicines | • Confirm whether there is a standard reporting form for substandard and falsified medical products used in your country, and whether it is independent of the standard adverse drug reaction reporting form.<br>• If there is a reporting form present, document the data fields. These could include:<br>  - product name<br>  - product type<br>  - registration or marketing authorization number<br>  - manufacturer<br>  - expiry date<br>  - adverse reaction reported or harm caused<br>  - outcome of adverse reaction<br>  - estimated number of patients affected<br>  - option to upload a photo if form is digital. |
| Guiding questions to explore data usage | • Is there a process in place for collection, recording and analysis of adverse drug reaction reports?<br>  - Does an information flow chain exist including causality assessments, feedback to reporters and an electronic database?<br>  - Are adverse drug reaction reports flowing into global databases (e.g. Vigilance hub, Vigiflow and Vigibase)?<br>  - What percentage of reports deemed satisfactory and submitted to the national pharmacovigilance centre were passed into global databases?<br>• Is there a process in place for collection, recording and analysis of substandard and falsified medical product reports?<br>  - Does an information flow chain exist including feedback for reporters and an electronic database?<br>  - Are reports of substandard and falsified medical products flowing into global databases (for example, the GSMS)? |
