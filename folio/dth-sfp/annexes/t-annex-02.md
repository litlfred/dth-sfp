| **What does success look like** | *Note: This does not have to be strictly professional success. An overall perception of success can greatly impact performance in a professional setting. Commitment to patient care, professional responsibility?<br>• Complete coverage of health care service providers?<br>• What indicators are they measured on?<br>• What are their performance management metrics?<br>• User-friendly (intuitive and easy navigation) platform?<br>• Availability in multiple devices (phone, tablet, desktop)?<br>• Feedback mechanism?<br>• Seamlessly integrated with other existing systems? |
