# Glossary of the Digital Transformation Handbook for Substandard and Falsified Medical Product Reporting Systems

Domain terms for substandard and falsified (SF) medical product reporting systems, quoting the WHO handbook (ISBN 978-92-4-012074-7) with L1 page provenance and SKOS mappings into dpi-h-ra (litlfred/smart-ra), dth-sc (litlfred/dth-sc), dth-pc (litlfred/dth-pc), and dth-hrh (litlfred/dth-hrh). General digital health and enterprise architecture terms are held in dpi-h-ra; supply chain and traceability terms in dth-sc; product master data and trade item terms in dth-pc; and health workforce / ISCO-08 occupation terms in dth-hrh.
